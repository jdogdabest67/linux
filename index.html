8. What the result means

Detected: only means the browser reports an API or a device in its chooser.
Not detected: does not mean the Joy-Con is broken. It may be connected only to the Switch, or Android may not expose it to this browser.
Even if the phone sees a Joy-Con, this page cannot automatically make the same Joy-Con send inputs to both the phone and Switch. We need an actual supported connection/bridge for that, and this test does not bypass Nintendo's controller pairing.

Privacy and safety: chooser actions require your tap. The interface and HID access tests may open a selected device, but they do not send controller commands or read input reports. The HID test only lists report-collection metadata and then closes the device.
